# Hotel Sherpa Soul — Owner CMS Manual
**For Owner Mr. Mingma Sherpa & Authorized Administrative Staff**

Welcome to your custom Content Management System (CMS) for Hotel Sherpa Soul. This manual provides simple, step-by-step instructions for managing your hotel website without editing any source code.

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Portal
1. Navigate to: `https://hotelsherpasoul.com/admin/login` (or `/admin` when logged in).
2. Enter your authorized email: `mingmasaino@gmail.com`.
3. Enter your secure password (default initial password: `SherpaSoul@2026!`).
4. Click **Enter Admin Dashboard**.
5. Once authenticated, a secure HTTP-only session cookie is created. You will remain logged in for 7 days on your device.

---

## 2. Navigating the CMS
The left sidebar gives you instant access to all core hotel functions:
* **Overview Dashboard:** Live occupancy % (based on your 6 sellable rooms), RevPAR, ADR, arrivals, departures, and recent inquiries.
* **Rooms & Inventory (6):** Manage which room category is assigned to physical rooms 201, 202, 203, 301, 302, and 303.
* **Direct Bookings:** View, manage, filter, and create direct reservations.
* **CMS & Website Content:** Edit hotel phone numbers, WhatsApp, business email, Meta Pixel ID, GA4 ID, special discount codes, and SEO blog articles.
* **Guest Inquiries:** Read messages submitted by travelers through your online contact form.
* **User Permissions (Owner Only):** Add or disable staff login credentials.
* **PMS Operations:** Access front-desk check-in, housekeeping task tracking, and billing.

---

## 3. Updating Hotel Contact Information
1. In the CMS sidebar, click **CMS & Website Content**.
2. Under the **General Settings & Trackers** tab, you will see fields for:
   * **Hotel Phone:** (e.g. `+977 9851068219`)
   * **WhatsApp Number:** (e.g. `9779851068219`)
   * **Business Email:** (e.g. `info@hotelsherpasoul.com`)
   * **Hotel Location:** (e.g. `Thamel, Kathmandu, Nepal`)
3. Make your desired text changes and click **Save Changes**. Changes appear instantly on the public website.
