# Hotel Sherpa Soul — Room & Inventory Management Manual

---

## 1. Strict Physical Room Inventory (Exactly 6 Sellable Rooms)
Hotel Sherpa Soul has exactly **6 sellable guest rooms**:
* **Floor 2:** Room 201, Room 202, Room 203
* **Floor 3:** Room 301, Room 302, Room 303

Do not create additional rooms in inventory.

---

## 2. Shared Kitchen (Area/Room 102) Rule
* Located on Floor 1.
* Dedicated facility for long-stay guests (minimum recommended stay 14 days).
* **CRITICAL:** Area 102 is NOT a guest bedroom. It is strictly excluded from sellable room inventory, occupancy %, ADR, and RevPAR formulas.

---

## 3. Physical Room Categories & Authoritative Rates
1. **Budget Family Room:** USD 20 / night. Maximum 3 Adults + 1 Child (Max 4 guests).
2. **Family Room:** USD 30 / night. Maximum 3 Adults + 1 Child (Max 4 guests).
3. **Deluxe Room:** USD 20 / night. Maximum 2 Adults + 1 Child (Max 3 guests).

---

## 4. Operational Formulas
* **Occupancy %** $= \frac{\text{Occupied Physical Rooms}}{6} \times 100$
* **RevPAR** $= \frac{\text{Total Room Revenue}}{6}$
* **ADR** $= \frac{\text{Total Room Revenue}}{\text{Sold Room Nights}}$
